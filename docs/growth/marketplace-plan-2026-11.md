# Marketplace launch plan — Bio Luxe Lab (November 2026)

Written 2 November 2026. Research and planning only: no accounts were created, nothing was listed, nothing was committed.
Catalogue figures come from the engine's `social_products` / `social_photo_audits` tables (read from a copy of the live DB taken 2 Nov, 11:13 GMT).
Every fee and rule below is tagged **[official]** (read on the platform's own page in this session), **[third-party]** (blog or calculator, treat as indicative) or **[unverified]**.

## 1. Bottom line

1. **Classic AliExpress dropshipping with 1–3 week delivery is allowed on none of the three marketplaces.**
   - **TikTok Shop UK:** you must hold stock in the country you register in, and Ship-by-Seller orders are expected to arrive within 4 working days. Direct-from-China parcels cannot meet either.
   - **eBay UK:** dropshipping from a *wholesale* supplier is fine. eBay's own page says buying from "another retailer or marketplace that ships directly to your customer is not allowed". AliExpress is a marketplace, so the literal reading rules it out.
   - **Etsy:** "Drop shipping is not allowed on Etsy", and reselling is not allowed. Production partners are only allowed for items you designed or the buyer customised.
2. **What works instead** is a small **held-stock model**. Buy 10–25 units of the best ~20 SKUs (from AliExpress or a UK-warehouse supplier), keep them at the registered Norwich address, and post them yourself with tracking. That also gives a 1–3 working day delivery promise, which should convert better than "3–14 days". Estimated working capital is £800–£1,500 [my estimate; needs real landed costs].
3. **Recommended order:** eBay UK first, then TikTok Shop UK, with Etsy limited to a few own-design or digital items. Amazon UK waits until there are real sales and barcodes.
   - eBay is the quickest to launch and has the least onboarding friction.
   - TikTok Shop pairs with the founder-video work already in progress, but cash arrives slowly (first payout window is 31 days after delivery).
4. **Realistic first-90-day numbers are small** (section 7). Base case across all three marketplaces is roughly 100 orders and ~£460 profit. Their value is conversion data and first reviews, not the £10k/month target. At ~£6 net per order, £10k/month needs ~1,650 orders a month.
5. **Correction to the brief:** Shopify's Marketplace Connect no longer takes new Etsy connections. It lists Amazon, eBay, Target Plus and Walmart (see section 4).

## 3. What each marketplace allows (shipping times and sourcing)

### TikTok Shop UK
- **Delivery clock (Ship by Seller):** confirm, pack and label within 2 working days. Hand to the carrier within 7 working days or the order is auto-cancelled. Delivery is expected within **4 working days**. A Late Delivery Fee applies when on-time delivery falls below 70% in a week, and it can apply even when the carrier causes the delay. Carriers must be TikTok-approved. **[official]**
- **Ship by TikTok** (default): hand-over within 2 working days of the order. **[official]**
- **Inventory:** must be stored in the UK, with "Local to Local" meaning shipped from and delivered within the UK. **[official]**
- **Dropshipping:** the UK pages I could load never use the word. Regional "Platform Abuse" policies seen only as search snippets (Vietnam, Thailand) ban fulfilling duplicated off-platform sales and falsified tracking, so treat that as low confidence. The practical position is clear: **stock in China with a 1–3 week delivery cannot meet the inventory or 4-working-day rules.** Ask Seller Support in writing before relying on any UK-warehouse supplier arrangement.
- **Restricted content:** weight-loss and fat-loss products and claims are unsupported. Claims that a product cures, treats or alleviates a condition are banned in titles, descriptions, images and videos. "Medical devices, or products that make medical claims" are unsupported. Fitness equipment is allowed if it makes no weight-loss claims. **[official]**
- **Images:** I could not load the official image rules. Treat printed claims, watermarks and text banners as rejection risks, since Google Merchant Center already flagged the same supplier images **[unverified for TikTok]**.

### eBay UK
- Dropshipping is allowed from a wholesale supplier. Purchasing after the sale from another retailer or marketplace that ships straight to your customer is not allowed. You remain responsible for delivery "within the time frame you stated in your listing". Penalties include listing removal, restrictions and suspension. **[official]**
- **Tracking:** upload it within your handling time. Funds for new or private sellers can be held until delivery is confirmed. **[official/third-party mix]**
- **Practical reading for this store:** hold stock in the UK, or use a genuine UK wholesaler with a pre-listing arrangement. Never list an item at a premium and then buy it from AliExpress to ship to the buyer.

### Etsy
- Items must be made, designed, sourced as craft supplies, or vintage (20+ years). Reselling ready-made commercial goods is not allowed, and neither is adding a card or note to unaltered goods. Production partners are allowed only for the seller's **original designs** or buyer customisation, and must be disclosed. **[official]**
- The only drop-ship exception is craft and party supplies. **[official]**
- **Result:** none of the 50 active products qualifies as sourced today. "Handmade" and "handcrafted" wording on supplier-made items would also misrepresent them on Etsy.

### Amazon UK (later option, not researched in depth)
- Professional plan ~£25 a month, referral fees roughly 6–17% by category, and 20% VAT on Amazon's fees **[third-party]**.
- I did **not** verify Amazon UK's dropshipping or seller-of-record rules or its GTIN exemption process **[unverified]**. Revisit after the first 90 days.

## 6. TikTok Shop affiliate and creator programme

- You pick the commission rate. Seller Center guidance allows **1–80%** and 0% is not allowed. TikTok suggests 10–15% for new products. **[official playbook, via search summary; the page itself would not load]**
- **Open Collaboration:** products appear in the Product Marketplace and any eligible creator can add them. **Target Collaboration:** you invite named creators at their own rate. Target rates override open rates. Edits to open rates can take about 30 days **[third-party]**.
- Commission formula: rate × (product price − seller discount − platform discount). It is deducted from each sale and paid to the creator automatically **[official]**. Creators are typically paid on day 3 or 15 after delivery depending on your settlement tier **[search summary of official page]**.
- Free samples are optional but encouraged **[official blog]**.
- **Margin check:** on a £25 item, 9% platform commission plus 10% creator commission leaves ~£4.75 after a 62% landed cost. Do not offer more than 10% on anything below ~£20, and offer 15%+ only on items above £30 with 40%+ margin.
- Creator posting limits on product-linked videos were tightened in January 2026 **[third-party]**.

## 7. Product selection

**How I ranked.** Price £12–£50 (impulse-friendly, low return cost), small and light parcels, not electrical where avoidable, no printed claims on photos, and category fit. Shopify-synced supplier stock is shown because stock under ~25 units causes seller-fault cancellations, which hurt both TikTok and eBay metrics.

**Demand signals are thin and external.** The engine has almost no usable internal data (973 metric rows, 54 impressions, 19 clicks). The external evidence is that TikTok Shop UK skews to beauty and skincare, with supplements, apparel and oral care next, and fitness mostly sold as apparel **[third-party, FastMoss/Plott Data]**. No source gave UK category rankings for yoga or wellness accessories. I did not price-check live comparables on eBay or TikTok. Do that before listing.

SELECT p.title,
       SUM(a.verdict='claim') AS claim_imgs,

FROM social_products p
LEFT JOIN social_photo_audits a ON a.product_id = p.product_id

**Decision thresholds (my suggestions):**
- TikTok: if there are fewer than 10 orders by week 8 and no creator accepts a sample, pause creator spend and keep only organic video.
- eBay: if listings average under 30 views a month by week 6, fix titles, item specifics and price against live comparables before adding listings.
- Both: pause any SKU with 2+ "not as described" or late-delivery cases. eBay adds up to +6 points of fees for sellers below standard.
