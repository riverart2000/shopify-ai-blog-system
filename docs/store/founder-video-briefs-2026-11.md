# Founder video briefs — November 2026

Ten videos, one per placement on the storefront and in the welcome emails. Each placement on the duplicate theme currently shows a placeholder image that reads "FOUNDER VIDEO GOES HERE · FV-xx", so a missing video is obvious on the page, never silent.

## How the videos reach the store (read once)

1. Record on the phone, landscape unless the brief says portrait. Keep the raw file; no editing needed.
2. AirDrop it to the Mac and drop it into `~/Bio Luxe Lab Videos` with the exact file name from the brief. The Social Engine picks files up every five minutes (`founder.scan`), publishes the full video to YouTube as a `founder_video` post, waits for the YouTube URL, then has Postiz cut clips and schedules them to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and Pinterest. Every post waits in the approval queue first (`founder_videos.approval_mode = manual`). Details: `docs/social-engine/founder-videos.md`.
3. The product link in the clip captions is matched from the words in the file name, so the file names below contain the kit or page title words on purpose.
4. Once YouTube reports the URL, swap the placeholder on the page for the video: in the theme editor open the section, delete the placeholder image block and add a **Video** block with the YouTube URL (or upload the file as a Shopify-hosted video for autoplay-muted hero use). For the emails, paste the YouTube link into the Shopify Email block. Rollback is the reverse: put the placeholder image back.
5. Recording limits: under 40 minutes per file (the engine refuses longer files), 120 clipping minutes a month on the Postiz plan, so keep the routine videos at the lengths below.

## Rules for every video (UK ASA/CAP and the store's own product data)

- No health, medical, weight-loss, body-shaping, pain, sleep-disorder or condition claims, about products, routines or coaching. Say what you do and how it feels, not what it fixes. Allowed: "I use this every evening", "this is the routine I actually follow", "it helps me switch off from the day". Not allowed: "cures", "treats", "fixes", "relieves pain", "improves sleep quality", "anti-ageing", "burns fat", "tones", "detox", condition names, percentages, "stronger at 58 than at 28".
- Nothing about a product that is not in its Shopify description: sizes, materials, what is in the box, how to use, delivery and returns only. If unsure, leave it out.
- The guarantee is worded exactly as the refund policy allows: 30 days from delivery, any reason, item unused and in its packaging, email info@bioluxelab.com to start, you pay return postage for a change of mind, we pay if faulty. Call it "The 30-Night Promise" and say "send it back for a refund", never "keep it and we refund".
- Delivery promise: "free UK delivery, dispatched in 1 to 3 working days, usually with you in 3 to 14 days". Never promise next day.
- No discount codes. The first-order bonus is the Routine Tracker pack and early access to the videos, "no code needed".
- Coaching and workshop videos: routines, habits, breathwork, meditation practice, sleep hygiene, training and recovery, measurement and accountability. Never outcomes for a condition. You may say you rebuilt your own routines in your fifties; do not name any condition or imply a cure.
- Keep supplier packaging with printed claims out of shot (several boxes carry wording the store cannot use).

## Setting and framing, shared

- Setting: your own living room or bedroom, lamp light, mat on the floor. The kit laid out in shot where it is the subject. Tidy, real, not styled.
- Framing: phone on a tripod at chest height, landscape 16:9, you a third from one edge so there is room for the product. Routine videos: phone low and wide so the whole mat is in frame.
- Sound: quiet room, no music (the engine adds none and music blocks clips on some channels). Speak slightly slower than feels natural.
- Clothes: plain, no logos, no slogans.
- Light: face the window or a lamp. Avoid the overhead light for the evening videos; the point is the lamp-lit room.
- Start every take with three seconds of silence looking at the lens, then the hook. End with two seconds of stillness after the call to action. It makes the clips cut cleanly.

Beats

6. 14:00 Close: "Phone stays out here. Goodnight." Two seconds of stillness.

Do not say: anything that sounds like a prescription ("this will", "you'll feel"). Say "I" and "if it feels good". Mention "stop if anything hurts" once near the start.

- Is the guarantee worded as thirty nights, send it back, refund?
- Is the file named exactly as above, and under 40 minutes?
