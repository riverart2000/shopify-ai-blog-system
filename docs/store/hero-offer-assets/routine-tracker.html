How to use it

Print the grid page (or print several). Write up to eight habits down the left, one per row. Each evening, colour the square for anything you did. Do not chase perfect rows: the aim is a grid that is mostly filled in, with gaps that do not turn into weeks.

At the end of each week, fill in the short reflection on the last page. At day 30, print a fresh grid, keep the habits that stuck and swap out the ones that did not.

Three rules that make tracking work

  • Anchor it. Attach the routine to something that already happens every day: after the dinner plates go in the sink, after the dog walk, when the kettle goes on.
  • Start smaller than you think. Two minutes of breathing you actually do beats twenty minutes you skip. Grow it once a row is mostly ticked.
  • Never miss twice. One missed day is noise. Two in a row is the start of a new habit called stopping. Make the next day the easiest version.
